## Product decisions (made by the product owner)
1. **Services live in the same catalog as products.** When adding a listing the seller picks **Product** or **Service**. Services share brands, title, photos, description, bullet points, category, Q&A/FAQ, policies, SEO, publish rules and the readiness checklist with products, but replace stock/shipping/variants with service sections. The catalog list gets a Type filter and shows services with their price model and duration.
2. **Each team member who provides services has their own calendar.** Services list which members can perform them; every booking is assigned to one provider. The account also has a combined calendar view.
3. **Kinds of services:** virtual (online), at the customer's location (e.g. comes to the house), and at the seller's location; priced **by the hour** or **by the job**.

## 1. Service listings (extend the products model minimally)
- `ListingType` (Product | Service) on the existing listing; service-specific data in its own entities/tables (e.g. ServiceDetails, ServiceProvider links) so product code stays untouched where possible.
- **Pricing model:** hourly (rate, minimum hours, billing increment e.g. 15/30/60 min, optional maximum), fixed price per job, or "quote required" (by-job work whose price depends on details; see quotes below). Optional add-ons (name + price or + time).
- **Duration and timing:** default duration (for hourly: default booked hours), buffer before/after, minimum notice (e.g. 24 h), maximum advance booking window (e.g. 60 days), slot granularity.
- **Where it happens:** Online (the seller adds a meeting link/instructions, revealed only to the confirmed buyer; auto-creating video meetings is out of scope), At customer's location (service area as a list of postal codes and/or a radius in km/mi from a base address; optional travel fee; travel buffer between at-home jobs), At seller's location (address from the brand/account). A service can offer more than one.
- **Booking mode:** instant booking, or request-to-book (seller must approve within a configurable time, e.g. 24 h, else auto-declined).
- **Intake questions** the buyer answers when booking (text / choice / yes-no; required flag), and requirements ("what to prepare").
- **Cancellation & reschedule policy:** free until X hours before, then a fee % (recorded and sent to the marketplace; money is handled by the marketplace), max reschedules; no-show policy.
- **Eligible providers:** which team members can perform it (members flagged as service providers), and assignment rule: specific member chosen by buyer, or auto-assign (least busy / round-robin).
- Publish rules for services: title, category, ≥1 image, price model complete, duration, at least one location type with its details (service area or address or online instructions), at least one eligible provider with availability.

## 2. Calendars and availability
- **Providers:** any active team member can be marked as a service provider (Owner/Admin decide; members can manage their own working hours). Each provider has a time zone (default: account time zone).
- **Working hours:** weekly schedule with multiple intervals per day; date-specific overrides (different hours on a date); time off / holidays (all-day or partial); manual blocks ("busy") with optional note.
- **Slot engine (server-side, single source of truth):** computes bookable start times for a service + provider (or any eligible provider) over a date range, applying working hours, overrides, time off, existing bookings and holds, buffers, travel buffer for at-customer jobs, minimum notice and maximum window. Correct across time zones and daylight-saving changes (store UTC; compute in the provider's zone; test DST transitions).
- **No double booking:** concurrency-safe at the database level (e.g. per-provider locking or an exclusion/overlap check inside a serializable transaction); two simultaneous requests for the same slot → exactly one wins.
- **Calendar export:** a private, revocable iCal (ICS) subscription feed per provider and per account (long random token, read-only, shows bookings with minimal buyer info by default). Two-way Google/Outlook sync and importing external calendars are out of scope (Roadmap) because they need OAuth credentials and fetching remote content.

## 3. Bookings (via the marketplace, reusing the orders integration)
- **Inbound (signed internal API, same HMAC scheme as orders):** availability query (slots for a service/date range/provider/location), **hold** a slot during checkout (expires, e.g. 10 min), **confirm** a paid booking (instant) or **request** a booking (request-to-book), buyer reschedule request, buyer cancellation, buyer messages (reuse conversations linked to the booking). Idempotent on marketplace ids.
- **Quotes (by-job / quote required):** buyer sends a quote request with details, address/area and preferred times → appears in the seller's Requests inbox → seller sends a quote (price, estimated duration, proposed time slots, expiry, note) → buyer accepts via the marketplace → booking created and slot reserved; or declines/expires.
- **Lifecycle (one state machine; invalid transitions → 409):** Hold → Requested (awaiting seller) → Confirmed → (Rescheduled) → In progress → Completed; side states Declined, Expired, Cancelled by buyer, Cancelled by seller, No-show. Seller actions: approve/decline request, propose new time, reschedule (with buyer consent event), cancel (reason, policy-based fee recorded), mark started, complete (for hourly: record actual time; if it exceeds the booked time, create an "additional charge request" event for the marketplace instead of changing the price directly), mark no-show, reassign provider (must be eligible and free).
- Every change goes to the marketplace through the existing **outbox** (signed, retried, dead-letter). Reminders to providers by email (day before + 1 h before, per-user preference); buyer reminders are the marketplace's job (send an event).
- **Simulator:** extend the dev simulator to query slots, hold/confirm/request bookings, request quotes, accept quotes, reschedule and cancel as a buyer. Testing/Development only.
- **Privacy:** the customer's address and phone are visible only to the assigned provider and Owners/Admins, and only from confirmation until N days after completion (config), then redacted. Online meeting links are revealed only to the confirmed buyer. Mask PII in logs.
